Privacy Policy

Chasewater Railway P-Way Hub

Last updated: 21 October 2026
About this version: This notice describes the current volunteer test version of the app. Shared photographs are not yet enabled; some information remains on individual devices. Requests about personal data and account removal can be sent to the contact address below.

Who operates this app

Chasewater Railway P-Way Hub is an internal maintenance coordination application for authorised members of the Chasewater Railway Permanent Way team. It is administered by the P-Way Hub app administrator for the volunteer team. Privacy enquiries, account access and deletion requests should be sent to: admin@chasewaterrailway.co.uk. Please contact this address if you have questions about the operation of the app or the handling of your personal data.

Who can use it

The app is intended for authorised volunteers and administrators. Access to shared registers requires an approved Firebase Authentication account. Different permissions apply to volunteer and Manager roles.

Information processed

  • Account information: sign-in email address, Firebase user ID, username, display name, role and active/inactive status. Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Maintenance information: patrol and inspection checks, defect reports and closures, work orders and updates, work requests, announcements, tools and consumables, and railway asset records. Entries may include names, account IDs, dates, descriptions, selected railway locations, mileage references and what3words words entered by users.
  • Photographs: optional photos captured or selected using the phone. In the current prototype these are retained locally on that device; they are not automatically uploaded to the shared Firebase register. Copies may also remain in the device camera/gallery or in files exported by the user.
  • Technical information: authentication and database providers necessarily process service requests and related technical/network information. When displaying map tiles, OpenStreetMap's tile service receives requests from the device, which can include its IP address and the tiles requested.

Why this information is used

The information is used to verify access, attribute inspection and work records, coordinate railway maintenance, show work progress and notices to authorised users, support administration and investigate problems. It is not used for advertising or sold to advertisers.

Sharing and service providers

Cloud account information is processed using Google Firebase Authentication, and shared text-based maintenance records are stored using Google Cloud Firestore. Records are visible to other approved P-Way Hub accounts according to their role. Map imagery is requested from OpenStreetMap; buttons may open Google Maps externally if a user chooses. We do not intentionally publish volunteer records for public access. Service providers may handle information under their own service terms and privacy notices.

Local storage and backups

This version saves some information in the app's local storage on the phone, including photographs, legacy data and reminders. Authorised administrators can export backups; exported copies are then subject to the security of the device or storage location chosen for them. Removing an app may delete its on-device data, but it does not automatically delete cloud records or separately exported backups.

Security

Access to the shared registers is restricted using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore security rules. Different privileges apply to Manager and volunteer accounts. Network requests to Google Firebase use secure connections. No digital service can guarantee absolute security. Volunteers should avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information in free-text fields, and should report lost devices or suspected unauthorised access promptly.

Retention, correction and deletion

Account details are held while a person is authorised to use the app, subject to necessary administrative and security records. Maintenance and inspection records, including archived records, may need to be retained for continuity, audit or applicable railway/legal requirements. The retention period depends on the type of record and relevant railway operational, legal and audit needs. The P-Way Hub administrator will review retention and deletion requests with the railway and will not retain personal data for longer than necessary for the applicable purpose. To ask for access, correction, account closure or erasure, contact admin@chasewaterrailway.co.uk. Requests will be reviewed with regard to any legal, safety or legitimate record-retention obligations. Where deletion is appropriate, the administrator must remove the relevant Firebase Authentication account, associated profile and eligible cloud records or identifiers; merely disabling login does not erase stored data. Device-local images and downloaded backups may require separate removal on each device or storage location.

Your choices

You can avoid attaching photographs and choose what descriptive information you enter into records, subject to the team's maintenance reporting needs. You can ask the administrator to correct your account details or remove your access. If you wish to stop using the app, contact the administrator to request account closure and any applicable data deletion.

Children

The service is intended for adult members of the authorised volunteer team, not for children.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app begins sharing photographs, adds analytics, changes its account processes or makes other material changes to data handling. The current version and date will be displayed here.
