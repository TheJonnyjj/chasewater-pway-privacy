Privacy Policy

Chasewater Railway P-Way Hub

Last updated: 25 October 2026
Restricted app — shared photos and reminders. Approved P-Way users can add maintenance records and JPEG evidence photographs. Photographs attached in the app are uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage and viewable by authorised accounts. Work Order notifications are delivered using Firebase Cloud Messaging when enabled on the phone.

To request removal of an account or personal data, see How to request account or data deletion below.

Who operates this app

Chasewater Railway P-Way Hub is an internal maintenance coordination application for authorised members of the Chasewater Railway Permanent Way team. It is administered by the P-Way Hub app administrator for the volunteer team. Privacy enquiries, account access and deletion requests should be sent to: admin@chasewaterrailway.co.uk. Please contact this address if you have questions about the operation of the app or the handling of your personal data.

Who can use it

The app is intended for authorised volunteers and administrators. Access to shared registers requires an approved Firebase Authentication account. Different permissions apply to volunteer and Manager roles.

Information processed

  • Account information: sign-in email address, Firebase user ID, username, display name, role and active/inactive status. Password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Maintenance information: patrol and inspection checks, defect reports and closures, work orders and updates, work requests, announcements, tools and consumables, and railway asset records. Entries may include names, account IDs, dates, descriptions, selected railway locations, mileage references and what3words words entered by users.
  • Photographs: users may optionally take or select photos for patrols, defects, work requests and job updates. When volunteers choose to attach a photograph in the app, it is uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage and is accessible to authorised volunteers using the app. Older photographs saved under earlier versions may remain only on the original device. Photos may also remain in the camera/gallery or in an export or backup made by the user.
  • Reminder device identifiers: where enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging registration tokens, random installation identifiers and timestamps are processed to send maintenance reminders to authorised devices.
  • Technical information: authentication and database providers necessarily process service requests and related technical/network information. When displaying map tiles, OpenStreetMap's tile service receives requests from the device, which can include its IP address and the tiles requested.

Why this information is used

The information is used to verify access, attribute inspection and work records, coordinate railway maintenance, show work progress and notices to authorised users, support administration and investigate problems. It is not used for advertising or sold to advertisers.

Assignments, activity reports and reminders

Work Orders can identify one or more authorised volunteers or Managers who are assigned to maintenance tasks. The Manager-only statistics screen summarises recorded contributions, including updates, reported defects, patrols, and work-order status. These figures are for coordinating maintenance and may not reflect all volunteer work. Shared reminder schedules and Manager-selected defaults are saved in Firestore. If phone notifications are enabled, the Android app processes a Firebase Cloud Messaging device registration token and an app-installation identifier. The token and identifier are stored in Firestore with the authorised account UID so scheduled reminders can be addressed to the correct devices. A scheduled Firebase function reads approved work-order assignments and due dates and sends generic reminders via Firebase Cloud Messaging. Notifications may appear on the device lock screen; maintenance details are viewed only after opening the app. Phone notifications are optional and only operate where the Android app and server notification service have been configured. In-app reminders do not require a phone notification token. Device registrations are removed on sign-out when online; users may request removal of stale registrations via the privacy contact.

Data protection and your rights

Personal information is handled to administer access to this restricted app and coordinate and document railway maintenance. These activities are generally undertaken for the legitimate interests of organising authorised railway maintenance and maintaining appropriate operational records, and for legal obligations where applicable. The railway administrator can explain which lawful basis applies to a particular request. Optional photo attachments and Android notifications can be avoided or disabled; disabling notifications does not remove existing maintenance records.

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, restrict or erase personal information, object to certain processing or raise a concern. Requests can be sent to admin@chasewaterrailway.co.uk. You can also make a complaint to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Sharing and service providers

Cloud account information is processed using Google Firebase Authentication, shared maintenance records are stored using Google Cloud Firestore, and photographs uploaded with records are stored using Firebase Cloud Storage. Records are visible to other approved P-Way Hub accounts according to their role. Newly uploaded photographs are stored centrally and accessible to authorised users on their devices, provided the upload succeeds. Map imagery is requested from OpenStreetMap; buttons may open Google Maps externally if a user chooses. We do not intentionally publish volunteer records for public access. Service providers may handle information under their own service terms and privacy notices.

Network and service access may involve technical identifiers and IP addresses. Requests to OpenStreetMap for map tiles may reveal the requesting device network address and approximate map area, and are subject to that service’s own privacy practices. Links to external map providers are optional.

Google services may process or store technical and account data in locations outside the UK under their applicable data-processing arrangements and safeguards.

Local storage and backups

The app may retain sign-in convenience information, map preferences and some historical device-only information locally. Newly attached photos are uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage if the upload succeeds; photographs captured with the camera may also remain in the device gallery. Work Order scheduling and reminder defaults are stored in Firestore, not solely on an individual phone. Older locally stored images may remain on devices running previous versions. Uninstalling the app does not erase shared Firebase records or photographs. Exports and copies made by users must be protected appropriately.

Security

Access to the shared registers is restricted using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore security rules. Different privileges apply to Manager and volunteer accounts. Network requests to Google Firebase use secure connections. No digital service can guarantee absolute security. Volunteers should avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information in free-text fields, and should report lost devices or suspected unauthorised access promptly.

Retention, correction and deletion

Account details are held while a person is authorised to use the app, subject to necessary administrative and security records. Maintenance and inspection records, including archived records, may need to be retained for continuity, audit or applicable railway/legal requirements. The retention period depends on the type of record and relevant railway operational, legal and audit needs. The P-Way Hub administrator will review retention and deletion requests with the railway and will not retain personal data for longer than necessary for the applicable purpose. To ask for access, correction, account closure or erasure, contact admin@chasewaterrailway.co.uk. Requests will be reviewed with regard to any legal, safety or legitimate record-retention obligations. Where deletion is appropriate, the administrator must remove the relevant Firebase Authentication account, associated profile and eligible cloud records or identifiers; merely disabling login does not erase stored data. Device-local images and downloaded backups may require separate removal on each device or storage location.

Accidental Work Order deletion

An authorised Manager can request permanent deletion of a Work Order that was created by mistake, after two confirmations. The deletion facility is restricted: in-progress, completed or closed work and records with progress updates, linked work requests or subsequent recurring work cannot be erased using this facility. Where permitted, the Work Order and its attached cloud photograph are removed by a protected server process. Deletion may be logged for security and audit. This facility is separate from an individual's right to request access to or erasure of their personal data using the contact below.

How to request account or data deletion

Users of Chasewater Railway P-Way Hub can request account closure, correction or deletion of personal information by emailing admin@chasewaterrailway.co.uk.

  • Use the subject P-Way Hub data deletion request and provide your username or the email address used for your account. Do not send your password.
  • State whether you want your account closed, personal data corrected, or eligible personal data deleted. The administrator may need to verify that the request is yours.
  • Where deletion is appropriate, the railway administrator will remove the Firebase Authentication account, associated user profile and eligible personal information from shared records.
  • Maintenance, safety, inspection or audit records may need to be kept where justified by applicable operational or legal obligations. Relevant records will be reviewed and retained only as necessary for those purposes; the applicable retention period depends on the record and obligation.
  • Photos saved only on a phone, in the camera/gallery or in files exported by a user are not automatically removed when a cloud account is deleted. Those copies must be deleted separately from the relevant device or backup location.

Contact the same address to request information about which records are held or retained and the reasons for retention.

Your choices

You can avoid attaching photographs and choose what descriptive information you enter into records, subject to the team's maintenance reporting needs. You can ask the administrator to correct your account details or remove your access. If you wish to stop using the app, contact the administrator to request account closure and any applicable data deletion.

Android notification permissions can be changed in your device settings. Revoking notification permissions prevents phone alerts; it does not by itself delete an account or records already submitted.

Children

The service is intended for adult members of the authorised volunteer team, not for children.

Changes

We may update this policy when app features, storage providers or record-handling procedures change. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
